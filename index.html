Why jaggery

Every other chai stall reaches for the sugar jar.

We don't. Every cup of Hyderabadi Amruttulya chai is sweetened with jaggery instead of regular sugar. It is the single thing that separates this chai franchise from the tea stall two doors down — and the thing customers taste first.

  1. 01

    Unrefined by design

    Jaggery is cane juice boiled down and set — not stripped white. It goes into the pot in place of refined sugar, in every cup we serve.

  2. 02

    A deeper flavour

    Dark, faintly smoky, closer to molasses. It holds its own against ginger, cardamom and a long boil, where white sugar simply disappears into the milk.

  3. 03

    A shorter supply chain

    Raw, unprocessed produce from farmers in rural India working by traditional methods and age-old wisdom, bought at fair prices.

What is Amruttulya chai?

“Amruttulya” (अमृततुल्य) means equal to nectar. It is chai served over a counter rather than at a table — strong, milky and spiced, boiled long. At Hyderabadi Amruttulya every cup of it is sweetened with jaggery in place of refined sugar.

Why jaggery instead of refined sugar?

Jaggery is cane juice boiled down and set, rather than stripped white. It is darker and faintly smoky, so it holds its own against ginger, cardamom and a long boil where white sugar disappears into the milk. It also comes straight from farmers in rural India, which keeps the supply chain short. It is the single thing that separates this chai from the tea stall two doors down.

Where do the tea and jaggery come from?

Raw, unprocessed produce comes from farmers in rural India who work by traditional methods and age-old wisdom, bought at fair prices, and every cup is sweetened with jaggery in place of refined sugar. The specific regions, and how supply reaches partners, are covered on the discovery call.
