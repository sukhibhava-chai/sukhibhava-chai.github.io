Why jaggery

Every other chai stall reaches for the sugar jar.

We don't. Every cup of Hyderabadi Amruttulya chai is sweetened with jaggery instead of regular sugar. It is the single thing that separates this chai franchise from the tea stall two doors down — and the thing customers taste first.

  1. 01

    Unrefined by design [Verify]

    Jaggery is cane juice boiled down and set — not stripped white. It goes into the pot in place of refined sugar, in every cup we serve.

  2. 02

    A deeper flavour

    Dark, faintly smoky, closer to molasses. It holds its own against ginger, cardamom and a long boil, where white sugar simply disappears into the milk.

  3. 03

    A shorter supply chain

    Raw, unprocessed produce from farmers in rural India working by traditional methods and age-old wisdom, bought at fair prices.

Our mission

Bridging the gap between urban and rural life

At Hyderabadi Amruttulya, we strive to bridge the gap between urban and rural life. Observing the challenges of urban living, we aim to address health concerns caused by processed and chemically produced products. Our mission is to offer you 100% natural products, delivered straight from the heart of rural India.

We are committed to providing you with raw, unprocessed and natural products, supporting a healthy and natural lifestyle. Our products are meticulously crafted by farmers using traditional methods and age-old wisdom.

“Amruttulya” means equal to nectar. That is the standard every pot is held to.

Our values

Rooted in health, farmers and a sustainable environment

Our values are deeply rooted in our commitment to promoting health, supporting local farmers and creating a sustainable environment. We believe in:

  • Health and wellness

    We offer 100% natural products, and bring you the purest ingredients we can source.

  • Empowering farmers

    We value our farmers and their traditional knowledge, paying fair prices for their produce and supporting sustainable practices.

  • Sustainability

    From sourcing to packaging, we work to minimise our environmental impact.

  • Community and collaboration

    We work closely with local communities to support their development and promote a sense of belonging.

  • Transparency and integrity

    Honesty and openness in every dealing, from sourcing through to customer service.

  • Innovation and excellence

    We keep improving our products and processes, and keep looking for better ways to work.
