Why jaggery

Every other chai stall reaches for the sugar jar.

We don't. Every cup of Hyderabadi Amruttulya chai is sweetened with jaggery instead of regular sugar. It is the single thing that separates this chai franchise from the tea stall two doors down — and the thing customers taste first.

  1. 01

    Unrefined by design

    Jaggery is cane juice boiled down and set — not stripped white. It goes into the pot in place of refined sugar, in every cup we serve.

  2. 02

    A deeper flavour

    Dark, faintly smoky, closer to molasses. It holds its own against ginger, cardamom and a long boil, where white sugar simply disappears into the milk.

  3. 03

    A shorter supply chain

    Raw, unprocessed produce from farmers in rural India working by traditional methods and age-old wisdom, bought at fair prices.

Our mission

Bridging the gap between urban and rural life

At Hyderabadi Amruttulya, we strive to bridge the gap between urban and rural life. Observing the challenges of urban living, we aim to address health concerns caused by processed and chemically produced products. Our mission is to offer you 100% natural products, delivered straight from the heart of rural India.

We are committed to providing you with raw, unprocessed and natural products, supporting a healthy and natural lifestyle. Our products are meticulously crafted by farmers using traditional methods and age-old wisdom.

“Amruttulya” means equal to nectar. That is the standard every pot is held to.

Franchise FAQ

Questions people ask before they call

How much does a Hyderabadi Amruttulya chai franchise cost?

The entry cost is deliberately kept low for first-time owners. The exact fee, what it covers, and the total setup cost including equipment and fit-out are quoted on your discovery call. Call +91 98499 98779.

What makes this an affordable chai franchise?

An outlet needs 100–200 sq ft and runs as a counter rather than a sit-down café, so both the space and the fit-out stay small. That is what keeps the entry cost of this tea franchise low. The exact fee is quoted on your discovery call.

Is a chai franchise profitable?

Hyderabadi Amruttulya outlets work on a 40%–50% profit margin. That figure moves with location, rent and running costs, so the numbers for your own site are worked through on the discovery call.

Do I need experience running a food business?

Most of our enquiries come from first-time business owners, and training is provided. What that training covers for someone with no food background is walked through on the discovery call.

How long does it take to open an outlet?

The clock runs from signed agreement to opening day, and covers site assessment, setup and training. The timeline for your city and your site is confirmed on the discovery call.

Do I get an exclusive territory?

Territory terms, how the radius is defined and which cities are currently open are confirmed directly with the franchise team on +91 98499 98779.

Where do the tea and jaggery come from?

Raw, unprocessed produce comes from farmers in rural India who work by traditional methods and age-old wisdom, bought at fair prices, and every cup is sweetened with jaggery in place of refined sugar. The specific regions, and how supply reaches partners, are covered on the discovery call.

What ongoing support do franchise partners get?

Training is provided before you open. What continues after that, and what it covers for your outlet, is set out on the discovery call.
